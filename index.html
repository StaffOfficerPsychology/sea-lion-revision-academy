SEA LION 2026 – Pre-Exercise Familiarisation Quiz

This is the compulsory recommended warm-up before commencing the exercise proper. It tests the General Idea, BLUE LAND Special Idea, key personalities and command appointments identified in the material, crisis chronology, political and military objectives, force components, operational constraints, adversary concepts, and principal learning outcomes.

Instructions: Attempt all 60 questions without consulting notes. Select one best answer for each. The answer key and explanations appear after submission. Aim for at least 16/20 (80%). This is a readiness check, not an official AFCSC assessment.
